## CS 466 - Project Product/Paper Grading
Grade: /50

Paper: Paper grade + Other grade
<br>
Program: Program grade + Other grade
<br>
Combo: (Program grade + Paper Grade)/2 + Other Grade


## Coding (40 pts)

| Earned | Possible | Requirement                                   | Grading Comments |
|--------|----------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------|
|    10   | 10        | Enough written code and enough comments and documents through out the codebase  |
|     5   | 5        |Code follows the design detailed in the progress report  |
|     5   | 5        | The implementation doesn't deviate much from what was proposed |
|     10   | 10        | Key parts  were implemented |
|     10   | 10        | Code is functional and the result can be easily replicated |
